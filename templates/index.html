Frequently Asked

Questions

We understand the importance of protecting your documents and ensuring a secure printing experience.
Below are some of the ways we safeguard your files and maintain your privacy.

01 How do we keep your documents safe?

All uploaded documents are stored temporarily and automatically deleted after printing. No files are retained on our servers beyond the printing process.

02 Who can access my documents?

To guarantee your privacy, the printing process is entirely automated and initiated exclusively by the system. No personnel or third parties have direct access to your file’s content at any stage. Your documents remain encrypted within our self-hosted server and are processed through a secured network, ensuring that your data is seen by no one but you before being permanently deleted after printing.

03 How is my document protected during upload?

Your document is encrypted and sent straight to our private self-hosted server where system is fully automated, meaning no humans can ever view the contents of your files. To ensure total privacy, your document is treated as a temporary file that is permanently deleted the second it finishes printing.

04 What happens if my document is not printed correctly?

If a printer error occurs, our system will automatically reroute your document to an active printer to ensure your job isn't stuck. For persistent issues requiring special assistance, our team will be notified for manual intervention. You may be prompted to re-upload only if a critical system error persists

05 Is my personal information stored?

We prioritize your privacy by storing only the essential session logs needed to manage the print queue. No sensitive data is collected or shared. In the rare event of a persistent error requiring a refund or special assistance, our team may request your contact details to resolve the issue. This information is used strictly for support and is not retained once the intervention is complete.

06 Do you track what I print?

Our system logs basic job details like the number of pages, color mode, and timestamp for queue management. However, document contents are not permanently stored or analyzed.

07 What is a CID and why do I need it?

A Customer ID (CID) is a unique reference number generated specifically for your print job. It acts as your digital ticket; you need it to authorize payment and to identify your documents in the printing queue. Without your CID, you cannot track or claim your printed files if the session is interrupted.

08 What should I do if I accidentally close the page?

Don't worry! Simply go to the Track Status tab on our homepage and enter your CID. Our system will instantly retrieve your current print status, showing you if your job is "Pending," "Processing," or "Ready for Pickup."

09 How do I use the "Track Status" feature?

The Track Status page works for both your Customer ID and Voucher Code. Enter a CID (e.g. CID-1234) to see real-time updates on your print job, including printer assignment and page progress. You can also enter a voucher code to instantly check your remaining credit balance and expiry date — no account needed.

10 Why am I seeing a different name during payment?

No cap, it's still us! To give you a seamless cashless experience without the corporate red tape, we use "CK French Perfume and Souvenir Shop" as our official payment gateway partner. Your payment is 100% secure and directly tied to your SafePrint job.

11 Can I give my leftover credit to a friend?

Absolutely! Your credit code isn't tied to any account, it's just a code. Share it with anyone and they can use it at checkout. Just make sure they enter it before paying!

12 Why do I have to pay ₱5 if my job only costs ₱2?

Our payment provider requires a minimum of ₱5 per transaction. Don't worry though, the extra ₱3 isn't lost! It's saved as credit on a voucher code shown on your confirmation page. You can use it on your next print job or share it with a friend.

About Us

Meet the skilled and experienced team behind
our successful privacy-focused print management system.

SafePrint is a secure and efficient print management system proudly funded by the Young LIFTers Program (YLP) Season 3 at Batangas State University, The National Engineering University. Developed as a college capstone project by Vincent P. Perez, Crystaline M. Datu, Lovely C. Guerra, and Jay E. Hosmillo. This project represents the innovation of Bachelor of Science in Information Technology and Bachelor in Industrial Technology students from the Alangilan Campus.

It aims to revolutionize the printing experience by providing a secure file transfer portal between customers and print shops. By utilizing a dedicated local server, the system ensures that documents are handled with maximum privacy and are never stored or left behind on public shop computers.
