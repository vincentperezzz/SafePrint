Frequently Asked

Questions

We understand the importance of protecting your documents and ensuring a secure printing experience.
Below are some of the ways we safeguard your files and maintain your privacy.

01 How do we keep your documents safe?

All uploaded documents are stored temporarily and automatically deleted after printing. No files are retained on our servers beyond the printing process.

02 Who can access my documents?

Only the system and authorized printing personnel can initiate the printing process. Personnel do not have direct access to your file's content unless you explicitly request editing assistance.

03 How is my document protected during upload?

We use secure encryption protocols to protect your document during upload, ensuring that your data remains private and secure.

04 What happens if my document is not printed correctly?

If there is a printing error (e.g., paper jam, misalignment), our system will notify our personnel for intervention. If necessary, you may need to re-upload your document.

05 Is my personal information stored?

We do not store any personal details beyond necessary session logs for managing the queue. No sensitive information is collected or shared.

06 Do you track what I print?

Our system logs basic job details like the number of pages, color mode, and timestamp for queue management. However, document contents are not stored or analyzed.

About Us

Meet the skilled and experienced team behind
our successful privacy-focused print management system.

SafePrint is a secure and efficient print management system developed as a college capstone project by Crystalline M. Datu, Charles Gabriel D. Gavino, and Vincent P. Perez taking BS Information Technology from Batangas State University The National Engineering University - Alangilan Campus. It aims to improve the printing process by providing a secure file transfer portal between customers and print shops, ensuring that documents are not stored or left behind on shop computers.
